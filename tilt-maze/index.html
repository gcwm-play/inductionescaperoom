Tilt Maze

Lay the phone flat inside the contraption before you start — the maze reads how far it's tilted from that resting position.

Tap Calibrate & Start, hold the tray level for a second while it zeroes, then tilt gently to roll the ball from the top down to the green goal. Avoid the holes — falling in resets the ball to the start.

No phone handy? Use the arrow keys (or WASD) on a keyboard to test the logic first.
