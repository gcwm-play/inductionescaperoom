Data Run

One of the data points is stored in a far-away storeroom.

Choose your fittest team member to "run" to the room and fetch it back!

Instructions:

  1. Choose your fastest team member to make the run.
  2. When ready, tap Start below and start walking or running, keeping the phone with you the whole way.
  3. Getting to the storeroom and back takes about 500 steps, so get moving!

Phone in a pocket not registering steps? Just hold it in your hand and give it a firm shake for each step instead.

No phone handy? Press the spacebar on a keyboard to test the step counter first.
